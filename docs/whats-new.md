- A new menu: the side drawer is now a grid of icons.
- The Builder and Recipes menus have tabs and swipeable cards with pictures. Items with several recipes let you pick one from the card.
- Entropy Surge: every watch now gives 10% of your net worth in entropy, or sometimes 10 crystals. No daily limit, just the 15-minute wait. It replaces Entropy Boost.
- New prestige upgrade, Free Research: the first research of each run finishes instantly.
- More levels for Assembly Line, Quantum Yield, Memory Resonance, Entropy Echo and every world's upgrades.
