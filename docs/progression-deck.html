What is it?

A factory-builder where the product is the universe itself.

You place machines on a grid. They harvest raw matter and craft it into more complex matter, which you sell for currency and reinvest into bigger, faster machines. It’s an idle game: play in short active bursts, and your factory keeps producing while your phone is in your pocket — you collect the earnings when you return.

The hook: you’re not making widgets. You’re climbing the actual ladder of matter, one scientific rung at a time.

The core loop · five verbs

Harvest, build, sell, research, reset.

1

Place machines on resource fields to pull in raw matter automatically.
2
Build & craft

3
Sell for Entropy
Feed surplus into a sink for Entropy, the game’s core currency.
4

Spend Entropy to unlock new machines, recipes, and whole new areas of the game.
5
Prestige (reset)
Once you’ve grown big, reset for a permanent speed-up — and climb again, faster. This is the heartbeat of the genre.
The big idea

One journey up the scale of matter — across three worlds.

Each world is its own map, its own resources, and its own tech tree. You unlock the next only after mastering the last. Then a cosmic-scale endgame caps it off.

World 1 · Particle Physics

Build matter from scratch.

You start with the universe’s raw ingredients — quarks and electrons — and bind them into protons, then atoms, then the whole periodic table, then simple molecules. This is where players learn the loop and take their first prestige resets.

Quarks→Protons & neutrons
Nucleons→Hydrogen & atoms
Atoms→The periodic table
Elements→Simple molecules
World 2 · Chemistry new

Mine the elements. Run the reactions.

A whole new map unlocks — and here the elements you painstakingly built in World 1 are simply dug out of the ground. Now the challenge is chemistry: combine them into salts, acids, and fuels, climbing all the way up to the first organic compounds — sugars and amino acids, the stuff of life.

Element fields→Oxides, salts, acids
Reactions→Industrial chemicals
Organics→Hydrocarbons & alcohols
Biochem→Sugars, amino acids
World 3 · Biology new

Assemble life itself.

The organic compounds from Chemistry become the raw material for a third map. Here you build proteins, cells, tissues, organisms — and finally whole ecosystems. It’s the top of the ladder that began with a single quark.

Organic fields→Proteins, DNA, lipids
Biomolecules→Cells & organelles
Cells→Tissues & organs
Organisms→Whole ecosystems
The endgame

Back to physics — at the scale of stars.

The long tail returns to the hardest physics: forging the heaviest and most exotic elements, then constructing a Dyson Sphere — a star-enclosing megastructure built in stages, each granting a permanent boost. This is the content players chase for hundreds of hours.

Why it works

A single, legible arc — from the smallest thing to the grandest.

Matter→ Chemistry→ Life→ Cosmos

There is always a next rung, the theme teaches real science as you climb, and the idle format fits a phone. Chemistry and Biology are the two new worlds that turn a single tech tree into a journey.
