Idle Builder · Whole-Game Progression Map · Draft v0.1

From Quarks to Life Three research worlds, one economy — and where every wall stands.

The game grows from one Particle Physics track into three stacked worlds. Physics builds the first elements; Chemistry opens mid-game to mine those elements and refine them into organic compounds; Biology opens later to grow compounds into life. Threading these through the middle lets the heavy-element / cosmic endgame move to the back, giving a clean arc instead of a front-loaded one.

Open questions & risks

Prestige payout

✦ scales flat-log (~+50✦ per ×10 net worth). Biology's 1B ceiling raises net worth but not the ✦ faucet — deep runs risk feeling ✦-starved. May need a higher prestige_scale or new faucets.

Gate costs are first-pass

Every cost here (150K / 500K / 2M / 5M / 10–25M / 50M / 250M / 1B) is a starting anchor off the phase table. This map exists so we can slide them together before committing numbers to game_data.json.

Field taxonomy

New element/organic fields need either a FieldType enum extension or a data-driven refactor. The refactor is cleaner long-term but touches more call sites — a Phase 2 decision.

Overlap to fix

Biology gates (50M–1B) currently overlap Megastructure's 50M. The deferred re-tier must push the heavy-physics endgame above Biology so the sequence reads cleanly.
