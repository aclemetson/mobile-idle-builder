Privacy Policy

Mobile Idle Builder, published by Clemtek Softworks
Effective date: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information Mobile Idle Builder (the "game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The game is currently in alpha testing. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at aclemetson12492@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, address or phone number, and we do not sell personal information. The game collects the following:

DataWhat it isWhy
Player ID A random identifier created by Unity Gaming Services when you first play. To keep your account, save your progress and link the data below to one player.
Google account link (optional) If you sign in with Google, a Google account identifier is linked to your player ID. We do not receive your Google password. To restore your progress on a new device.
Game progress Your save data (buildings, resources, research, purchases of in-game items, settings). Cloud save, so progress survives reinstalls and device changes.
Gameplay analytics Game events and periodic snapshots of game state (for example currency totals, buildings placed, research completed, prestige runs, play time, and whether developer tools were used). Collected only during announced testing phases. To balance the game's economy and find where players get stuck.
Crash and diagnostic reports Error logs and crash reports with device model, operating system version and app version. To find and fix bugs.
Advertising ID Your device's resettable advertising identifier, plus basic device and network information, collected by our ad provider when you choose to watch a rewarded ad. To serve and measure ads, prevent ad fraud, and (where allowed) personalize ads.
Purchase records Records of in-app purchases (product, time, transaction ID). Payment details are handled by Google Play; we never see your card number. To deliver what you bought and handle refunds.
Feedback you send If you use "Send Feedback", your email address, your message, and the app version and player ID that the game adds to the email. To reply to you and investigate the report.

Third-party services

The game uses the following providers, who process data on our behalf or under their own policies:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Analytics, Remote Config, Cloud Diagnostics): Unity privacy policy
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it mediates, for rewarded ads: ironSource privacy policy
  • Google Play Billing for in-app purchases, and Google Sign-In for optional account linking: Google privacy policy

Advertising

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

How long we keep data

  • Account and cloud save data: for as long as your account exists, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics events: up to 13 months (the Unity Analytics retention limit), then deleted.
  • Crash reports: kept as long as needed to fix the issue, normally no longer than 12 months.
  • Feedback emails: kept until the issue is resolved.

Your choices and deleting your data

To access or delete your data, email aclemetson12492@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Include your player ID if you can (the game adds it to feedback emails). We will delete your account, cloud save and associated analytics records within 30 days and confirm by email. Uninstalling the game removes local data from your device but does not delete cloud data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent between the game and our providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reputable providers and limit what we collect.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, they rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. We will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, give notice in the game.
