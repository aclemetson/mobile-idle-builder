Quantum Foundry — Crystal Economy & Time Warp

Rebalance of the crystal (◆ paid currency) economy + the new Time Warp sink. Source of truth: PremiumShopCalculator.cs, docs/agents/economy-balance.md. · PR #83
The value anchor
$1 ≈ 4 hours of progress  →  150◆ = 1 "skip-hour"

Crystals buy time, not raw power. The non-time sinks (entropy, prestige currency) grant a % of the current build, so their real-money value self-scales as the player progresses. Skips are priced to feel earned — a dollar buys a meaningful chunk of an idle day, not a whole one.

🧩 Why we did this

The four IAP packs were being finalized in Google Play Console, but the crystal economy had been tuned in three disconnected pieces — the free faucet, the sinks, and the pack sizes — with no consistent idea of what a crystal is worth. Three concrete problems:

  • Faucet too generous: a completionist earned ~5,000◆/month free — more than the $4.99 pack.
  • Sinks mispriced: the priciest single sink cost more than the $4.99 pack delivered, and the prestige grant was a flat amount that ignored progress.
  • No unifying model: speed-ups, entropy, and prestige currency sat on unrelated scales.

⏩ New sink: Time Warp

Instant offline collection — the purest expression of the anchor. Banks N hours of offline production at the player's current rate with no waiting (unlike the speed boost, which you wait out). New "⏩ Warp" tab in the in-game shop.

TierBanksCost
Time Warp I2 hours of production350
Time Warp II8 hours of production1,300
Time Warp III24 hours of production3,500

How a purchase resolves (PremiumShopService.TryBuyTimeWarp):

  1. Validate affordability (pure, cheap) before touching save/ECS.
  2. SaveLocal() — refreshes the idle snapshot from the live world so the payout reflects current production.
  3. OfflineCollectionService.ComputeForDuration() — computes N hours of payout at the idle collection rate, no cap, no mutation.
  4. If nothing is producing → NothingToCollect, the player is not charged.
  5. Deduct crystals; apply entropy + items to the live ECS world (AddEntropy / new AddInventoryItems); SaveLocal() to persist.

It reuses the exact offline-collection math — Time Warp pays precisely what N hours offline would, so there is no separate balance model to keep in sync.

💧 Faucet (free crystal income)

The 6× anchor increase already tightened the real economy hard (a completionist's free income now buys ~33 skip-hours instead of ~200), so the faucet needed only a light touch. We fixed the one structural flaw: daily achievements and daily challenges double-paid the same actions (craft / research / spend / login / prestige).
